A note for my Helene.

— Letter Nº 02 · From Toto —

My dearest Helene — what a year it has been. Truly, what a beautiful, full, joyful year. We did so many things together, didn't we?

Belgium was wonderful. Disneyland was wonderful. Wandering through Belgium with you — every small street, every small moment — was just as wonderful.

And then Mayu and Papu came all the way to Japan, and we got to show them so many of the places we love. We watched a quiet pile of movies, just the two of us, just the four of us. And this was the year your friends finally made it to Japan too — the house full of laughter we hadn't heard in too long.

I'm certain — between all of us — we made more happy memories this year than in any year before it. Thank you. For everything. For all of it.

— with all my love,
your Toto.
P.S. Happy thirty-second, mon amour.
